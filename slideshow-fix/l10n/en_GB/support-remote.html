Support is just one click away

When you need help, there's no need to explain the problem over the phone or wait for a visit. Solwed OS comes with RustDesk, ready to go: open it from the start menu, share the ID and password it shows you, and our support team connects straight to your computer — only with your permission, never without it.

We run our own server instead of RustDesk's public one, so connections are faster and don't depend on anyone else. It's like having someone from support sitting right next to you, without them having to travel.
