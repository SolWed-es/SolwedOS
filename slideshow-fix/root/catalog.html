Everything you need, already installed

Solwed OS comes with the tools your business needs from day one: LibreOffice for office work (Writer, Calc, Impress...), Thunderbird with WhatsApp built in so email and messaging live in one place, and FacturaScripts for managing and invoicing your business.

For paperwork with the Spanish administration it includes Autofirma and Okular, with digital PDF signing built in. And with Timeshift, you can always create a restore point and roll back if something goes wrong — like Windows' "System Restore", but on Solwed OS.
